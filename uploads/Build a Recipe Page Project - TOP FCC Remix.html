Creamy Chocolate Fudge

Description

This creamy chocolate fudge is rich, smooth, and simple enough for a beginner recipe page.

It uses basic pantry ingredients and sets into sweet squares that are easy to share.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Line a small pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the chocolate chips, condensed milk, and butter together.
  3. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Spread the mixture into the pan and let it set before cutting.
