bear with me, please. i promise this home page
will be extremely temporary. i just felt what i want to say
deserved to live here if only for a moment.

between impending eviction hanging over my life,
suicidal thoughts racing for a straight week on end,
988 had become a call too regular, too many nights.

yes, folks... i was quite low. i admit that,
and i do wholeheartedly admit it.
i am not ashamed of such happenings;
fucking FAR from ashamed, in fact.

yet even so, to lack motivation to be,
getting through, get on a worn struggle-bus,
entire body, whole mind, faint soul,
crawling through proverbial sludge, inch-by-inch,
my entire existence stuck to molasses floorboards,
barely any wherewithal just to simply respire...

befallen the seed.
resurgences;
alas, tenacity.
