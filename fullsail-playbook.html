the full sail playbook v.1

Web Development, Bachelor of Science — online. 27 months, 108 weeks, 120 credit hours.

six modules, each closing on a Project & Portfolio course. those checkpoints are Full Sail's own placement, not mine.

i don't set this pace. a module opens when the calendar opens it. the sequence isn't mine to reorder — what i control is how hard i lean in, and what comes out the other side.

rules · read these when overwhelmed

  1. the deadline is the deadline. this calendar doesn't flex
  2. syllabus in week one. know how it's graded before it's due
  3. ask the first time it's unclear, not the third
  4. bad month? tell an advisor while it's still fixable
  5. the P&Ps carry the portfolio. never coast one
  6. no filler courses. all 120 hours count
  7. the sequence is theirs. lean harder, don't reorder
  8. show up. the degree is the deliverable
  9. keep every graded project. month 27 pulls from all of them

definition of done (per module)

— the Project & Portfolio deliverable exists and is visible.
— this page reflects what actually happened.
— at least one obsidian nightly report exists for the stretch.
