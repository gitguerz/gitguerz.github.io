about

hi, i’m guerz.

my roots are in the y2k internet — messing with deadjournal and later livejournal layouts, tweaking the frontend aesthetics until it felt like mine. this isn’t a beginner story. it’s a return. the muscle memory never left. it was just waiting for the right moment to come back online.

this is my little corner of the internet, where i build, write, learn, and try to turn the weird little fractures in my life into something sturdier and more useful.

i’m a self-taught web developer, dj, blogger, and all-around internet gremlin with a bad habit of caring too much and doing things the hard way. i’m also trying to break generational patterns, honor the people who came before me, and build a life that doesn’t just survive the past — it outlives it.

guerz.lol is part portfolio, part brain dump, part ongoing proof that i’m still here and still making things. it holds my projects, my writing, my study notes, and whatever else feels worth dragging into the light.

the vibe here is honest, a little dark, occasionally funny, and definitely not sterile. i like websites that feel lived in. i like art that remembers being made by a person. i like progress that actually means something.

if you’re here, thanks for stopping by. i’m probably building the next thing right now.
