How these tools were made

Every tool in the Toolshed was made with AI. I came up with the idea for each one, decided what it needed to do, and tested it until it worked the way I wanted. The code itself was written mostly by two AI assistants: Plexi (Perplexity) and Bonnie (Claude).

  • Me (Guerz): the idea, the requirements, the testing, and the final call on what ships.
  • Plexi (Perplexity): research, first drafts, and most of the original builds.
  • Bonnie (Claude): fixes, revisions, quality checks, and fitting each tool into this site.

That makes the Toolshed different from the rest of guerz.lol. Everything in the FCC Build Archive and the Sandbox is coded by me, by hand, as part of my learning. The Toolshed holds tools I use for my own work, built by directing AI instead of writing every line myself.

Each tool also has a liner note on its own page with the details.
